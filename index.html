Department of Biology · United Arab Emirates University

Reading the unseen biology of arid ecosystems.

The MEEG lab studies how microbiota shape environmental, plant, animal, and human health — and how they hold ecosystems together under heat, salinity, and global change.

Al Ain, UAE PI: Dr. Sunil Mundra Microbial ecology · Multi-omics

“In a single gram of desert soil lives a community more diverse than the forest above it — and far less understood.”

01 · Research themes

What we study

Arid agroecosystems

Date palm & desert microbiomes

Mycorrhizal and endophyte interactions, soil-plant-microbe networks, and the truffle–Helianthemum symbiosis under arid conditions.
Animal microbiomes

Camel gut & milk microbiome

Functional analysis of microbial communities in camels — a uniquely adapted desert host — and what they reveal about resilience and nutrition.
Climate & change

Salinity, drought & belowground carbon

How global change pressures — especially salinity and drought — reshape belowground microbial communities and carbon dynamics.
One Health

Wastewater & antimicrobial resistance

Sewage microbiomes across Abu Dhabi as a window into the emergence and spread of AMR genes from different wastewater sources.
Cross-biome

Forests, mangroves & polar soils

Comparative work spanning arid, forested, mangrove, and Arctic biomes to ask what microbial life does for ecosystems everywhere.
Methods

Multi-omics in the field

Metabarcoding, metatranscriptomics, metaproteomics, and whole-genome sequencing — bringing genomics-scale tools to ecological questions.
