date: 2025-04-12

Our paper on latent variable models for high-dimensional single-cell data is out today in *Nature Methods*. The work, led by Alex Smith with help from Jane Lee, introduces a hierarchical prior structure that lets us share information across related cell types — improving estimates of rare populations without distorting common ones.
