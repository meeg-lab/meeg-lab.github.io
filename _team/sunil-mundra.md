I am working as an Associate Professor at the Department of Biology at United Arab Emirates University (Al Ain, Abu Dhabi, UAE). I hold a PhD in Molecular Microbial Ecology from the University of Oslo (UiO, Norway). Currently, I am serving as the Principal Investigator of the “Microbial Ecology and Environmental Genomics” lab, which is engaged in cutting-edge research to comprehend the impact of climate and environmental changes on soil-plant-microbial tripartite interactions in diverse environments ranging from forests, polar regions, to arid regions. 

My lab research interests are multifaceted and encompass identifying the intricate relationships between microbial diversity and ecosystem or host function. Lab employs state-of-the-art genomic and multi-omics approaches, including metagenomics analyses, microbial culturing, and machine learning to accomplish these objectives. Furthermore, our lab research vision extends beyond fundamental research to practical applications that can potentially improve agricultural productivity and environmental sustainability, with a particular focus on the arid environment. I have published more than 80 research papers in leading microbiology and ecology journals and supervised several Master’s and PhD students at UAEU. Additionally, I have has been the recipient of the prestigious Abu Dhabi Young Investigator Award.
