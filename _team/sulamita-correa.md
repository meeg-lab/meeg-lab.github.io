Dr. Sulamita Correa is a microbial biotechnology researcher focused on plant-associated bacteria, biological nitrogen fixation, and sustainable solutions for crop productivity. Her work spans endophytic and symbiotic microbes, including studies on stress alleviation in crops such as tomato, cowpea, and other legumes, as well as the broader roles of beneficial bacteria in plant growth and resilience.

She also has research in carbon fixation and microbial metabolism, reflecting a strong interest in using microbial systems to support both agricultural and environmental biotechnology. Overall, her profile suggests a researcher bridging plant-microbe interactions, stress tolerance, and applied biotechnology for sustainable agriculture.
