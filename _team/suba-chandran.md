Suba received her Master's degree in Biotechnology from Kerala University, India, and joined the MEEG lab in Fall 2020.

She investigated the impact of saline water irrigation on the fungal community associated with date palm (*Phoenix dactylifera*) in UAE agroecosystems using amplicon sequencing. She has also evaluated salinity stress-induced functional responses of microbiome communities using meta-transcriptomics.
