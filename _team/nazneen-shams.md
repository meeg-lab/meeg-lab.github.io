As a Master's student in Environmental Sciences and Sustainability, I am investigating the synergistic effects of salicylic acid (SA) and a synthetic microbial community (SynCom) on salt tolerance in tomato (Solanum lycopersicum). My research examines how the combined application of SA and plant growth-promoting bacteria can mitigate the damaging effects of salinity stress, with a focus on photosynthetic performance, antioxidant defense mechanisms, ion homeostasis, and stress-responsive gene expression. Conducted under controlled greenhouse conditions, this work aims to uncover the molecular and physiological basis of SA–SynCom synergy as a sustainable, low-input strategy for improving crop resilience in saline environments.
