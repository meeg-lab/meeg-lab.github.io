Dinesh investigates the microbiome and functionality of both natural ecosystems (mangrove, desert, and Arctic tundra) and engineered ecosystems (anaerobic biodigesters). His current research questions focus on the effects of irrigation (saline vs. non-saline water) and compartmental effects (root-soil-leaf) on microbial communities of date palm (*Phoenix dactylifera*) rhizospheres.

His prior research linked diversity and function in mangroves, examined sludge microbiomes and biological ammonia removal from urine wastewater, and explored microbial ecology and biogeochemistry of lake, tundra, and fjord ecosystems in the Arctic.
