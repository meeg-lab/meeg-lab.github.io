Dr. Amina Ilyas is a plant scientist specializing in plant–microbe interactions, with a PhD from the University of Paris-Saclay (France). Her research focuses on harnessing beneficial microbial communities to enhance plant nutrition, health, productivity, and resilience. At the MEEG Lab, she works on developing sustainable biocontrol and biostimulant solutions for agriculture. Her projects include designing microbial-based strategies against fungal pathogens, particularly in date palm, using beneficial microbes, their secondary metabolites, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). She also investigates mycorrhizal diversity and their role in plant nutrition and health, as well as the potential of beneficial bacteria to promote plant growth under low nitrogen conditions. Looking ahead, her research explores plant–microbe interactions under combined environmental stresses and elevated CO₂ to better understand their role in climate-resilient agriculture.
