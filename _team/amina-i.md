Dr. Amina Ilyas is a plant science researcher whose work centers on crop improvement, plant growth promotion, and sustainable agriculture. Her publications span plant heterosis, chickpea disease resistance, nutrient management, and beneficial microbe–plant interactions, showing a strong interest in improving crop productivity and resilience.

Her recent research also highlights mechanistic work on Enterobacter sp. SA187 and its effects on Arabidopsis growth under low nitrogen and elevated CO2, suggesting a focus on how microbes influence plant signaling and metabolism. Overall, she appears to combine applied agronomy with molecular plant science, bridging field-level performance with lab-based biological mechanisms.
