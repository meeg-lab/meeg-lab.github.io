Wastewater is a critical reservoir for the emergence and spread of antimicrobial resistance (AMR). We are characterizing AMR gene profiles across different wastewater sources in Abu Dhabi to understand how human, agricultural, and clinical practices contribute to AMR proliferation.

## Current questions

- How do AMR gene profiles differ between domestic, hospital, and industrial wastewater?
- Which bacterial taxa carry the most clinically relevant resistance genes?
- Does wastewater treatment effectively reduce AMR loads?

## Methods

Combining metagenomic sequencing of wastewater samples with whole-genome sequencing of multi-drug resistant bacterial isolates to characterize resistance mechanisms and mobility.
