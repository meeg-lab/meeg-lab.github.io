The air around us is alive with bacteria, fungi, and viruses, yet its microbial composition remains one of the least-studied frontiers in microbial ecology. We are characterizing airborne microbial communities in arid environments and indoor settings to understand their dynamics, sources, and implications for ecosystem and human health.

## Current questions

- What is the baseline composition of airborne microbial communities in arid UAE environments?
- How do dust storms, urbanization, and seasonality reshape the air microbiome?
- Do airborne microbes contribute to AMR dispersal?

## Methods

High-volume air sampling combined with amplicon sequencing and culture-based isolation of airborne bacteria and fungi.
