Observational data is abundant — but the assumptions required to interpret an association as a causal effect are strong, and rarely fully testable. We work on methods that make those assumptions clearer, weaker where possible, and easier to probe for sensitivity.

Current threads include doubly-robust estimation under interference, sensitivity analysis for unmeasured confounding, and methodology for target trial emulation in clinical research.
