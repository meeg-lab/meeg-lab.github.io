Arid soils are often dismissed as biologically simple, but a single gram can host thousands of microbial species working under extreme heat, water limitation, and salinity. We are characterizing the bacterial, fungal, and archaeal communities of UAE arid soils to understand how they sustain ecosystem processes in conditions where most organisms cannot persist.

## Current questions

- How do microbial communities differ across arid soil types — sandy desert, hypersaline sabkha, gravel plains, and biological soil crusts?
- Which microbial functions support nutrient cycling, carbon storage, and plant productivity in water-limited soils?
- How do extreme temperature and salinity gradients shape community assembly?
- What is the diversity of stress-tolerant microbes that could be applied in sustainable agriculture?

## Methods

We combine field sampling across the UAE with metabarcoding (16S, ITS), shotgun metagenomics, and culture-based isolation of stress-tolerant taxa.
