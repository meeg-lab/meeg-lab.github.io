Mangroves are among the most productive ecosystems on Earth, and the UAE coastline hosts important *Avicennia marina* stands. The microbial communities buried in mangrove sediments drive nitrogen cycling, sulfur cycling, and methane dynamics, but they remain poorly characterized in the Arabian Gulf.

## Current questions

- How do microbial community composition and function vary across UAE mangrove sites?
- What roles do these communities play in carbon storage and biogeochemical cycling?
- How do urbanization, salinity, and pollution shape mangrove microbiomes?

## Methods

Sediment cores are processed for amplicon sequencing and shotgun metagenomics, paired with environmental measurements (salinity, organic carbon, redox conditions).
