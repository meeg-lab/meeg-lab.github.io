The date palm (*Phoenix dactylifera*) is a keystone species of UAE agriculture, but groundwater used to irrigate it is increasingly saline. We are studying how this salinity stress reshapes the microbial communities living on and around date palm roots, and what those changes mean for plant health and soil function.

## Current questions

- How does saline groundwater irrigation alter bacterial and fungal community composition in date palm rhizospheres?
- Which microbial taxa are sensitive vs. resilient indicators of irrigation salinity?
- Do endophytes and mycorrhizal fungi help date palms tolerate salinity stress?
- How do these microbiome shifts affect carbon and nutrient cycling in arid agroecosystems?

## Methods

We combine field sampling at date farms across the UAE with metabarcoding (16S, ITS), metatranscriptomics, and functional gene analyses to characterize community structure and activity.
