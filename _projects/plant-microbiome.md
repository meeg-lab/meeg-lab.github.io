Plants live in intimate association with diverse microbial communities — bacteria, fungi, and archaea that colonize roots, leaves, and internal tissues. These microbiomes can dramatically influence plant nutrition, stress tolerance, and disease resistance. We study how plant microbiomes assemble across UAE crop and native plant species, and how they can be harnessed to improve agricultural sustainability in arid environments.

## Current questions

- How do compartments — root, rhizosphere, endosphere, and phyllosphere — differ in microbial composition and function?
- Which microbial taxa promote plant tolerance to salinity, drought, and heat stress?
- Can synthetic microbial communities (SynComs) be designed to enhance crop performance in saline soils?
- How do mycorrhizal partnerships shape native arid plant communities?

## Methods

Field sampling across UAE crop systems and native vegetation paired with amplicon sequencing, metagenomics, culture-based isolation, and greenhouse trials with synthetic microbial communities.
