This course introduces graduate students to modern computational biology workflows, from raw sequencing reads to interpreted results. We emphasize **reproducibility** throughout — every assignment is built around version-controlled, containerized pipelines that students can rerun end-to-end.

## Topics

- Unix, shell scripting, and HPC fundamentals
- Read alignment and variant calling (BWA, GATK)
- RNA-seq quantification and differential expression (Salmon, DESeq2)
- Single-cell RNA-seq (Scanpy, Seurat)
- Workflow management (Snakemake, Nextflow)
- Reproducibility (Docker, Conda environments, version control)

## Prerequisites

Basic programming (any language) and a course in molecular biology, or equivalent experience. Students without prior shell experience should complete the [Software Carpentry Unix shell lesson](https://swcarpentry.github.io/shell-novice/) before week 1.
